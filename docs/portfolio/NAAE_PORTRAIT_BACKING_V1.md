# Translucent portrait backing v1

The owner's clarification supersedes the previous glyph-opacity-only treatment: use an actual translucent backing below the face and shoulders, black in dark mode and white in light mode. At the owner's subsequent request for more strength, the selected levels are **82.5% black** and **93% white**, a 50% relative increase over 55% / 62% (initial comparison: 42% / 50%).

The `portrait-frame::before` layer sits beneath the unchanged glyph image. Its alpha mask is generated from the existing source-derived portrait envelope, fitted with the same contained aspect ratio as the image. An 18-source-unit feather softens the outline; vertical alpha stops keep hair backing at 85–90% of the face level and fade the lower shoulders. Hair was subsequently strengthened from the earlier 18–35% levels at the owner's request. Percentages are maximum backing opacity, not a uniform opaque silhouette. There is no rectangular fill beyond the masked shape, circle, glow or facial outline. The foreground SVG, font, text, copy, layout and navigation are unchanged.

The previous incremental environmental-opacity increase was returned from 0.76 to its V3 value of 0.68 to avoid stacking excessive attenuation. The backing now supplies the additional separation across all forest colours. Light mode explicitly uses white rather than darkening its background glyphs.

The 1,667-byte mask is generated by the existing portrait build script. It adds no runtime JavaScript, canvas or per-frame image analysis. The entry remains 214,359 raw bytes under the unchanged 215,000-byte budget.

Validation: typecheck, lint, production build/prerender and 185 full tests passed at the initial backing levels. After the final CSS-opacity increase, the build and all nine portrait tests passed again. The 55%/62% browser captures cover dark/light desktop, mobile, Arabic RTL and Japanese vertical. After the final increase to 82.5%/93%, dark/light desktop and mobile were recaptured; exact results, retries, hashes and route audit are in `verification/naae-portrait-backing-v1.json`. Earlier 42%/50% captures are retained alongside final evidence in `output/playwright/portrait-backing-v1/`.

This remains a subjective visual adjustment. No new participant, screen-reader or physical-device study was performed. No commit, push or deployment. Owner work was preserved.

Hair refinement: the mask stops at the top and upper hair region are now 0.85 and 0.90, rising to 1.0 at the face. The 18-unit outline feather, shoulder fade and 82.5%/93% theme opacities are unchanged. Build and nine focused portrait tests passed again; fresh dark/light/mobile captures are retained under `hair-refined/`.
