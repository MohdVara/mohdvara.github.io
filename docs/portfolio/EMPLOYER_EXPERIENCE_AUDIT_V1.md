## Overall assessment

**SIMULATED JUDGMENT:** This is a persuasive senior hands-on engineering portfolio with unusually candid and inspectable independent technical work. It presents best for modernization, frontend/internationalization, creative technology and senior full-stack conversations. It is less conclusive for big-tech principal leveling, enterprise people management or production AI leadership because commercial scope, independently corroborated impact and live operational evidence are thinner than the independent-project documentation. The linked résumé introduces avoidable consistency questions that should be resolved before adding more features.

The first desktop viewport is clear enough to continue: role, location, business focus, résumé and contact are visible. The artwork is distinctive without obscuring those actions. At 390×844 the page becomes a vertical stack but the primary actions and résumé remain reachable and visible. NAAE is discovered lower in public engineering; PACE and NTM have hero shortcuts. This means a technically excellent deep case cannot automatically improve a recruiter’s initial screen.

## Methodology and evidence

Weights, expectations, planned journeys and initial notes were saved in `predeclared-rubrics.json` before deep case investigation and public source reads. Pass A used the clean English-home snapshot and desktop screenshot only. All twelve initial interpretations were locked before Pass B. Full prose was expanded during reporting from those notes; no later case-study result is credited to Pass A. The home snapshot includes offscreen DOM content, but the initial reactions credit only the visible 1440×900 opening. Mobile evidence was collected afterward.

This is twelve distinct evaluation passes over one evidence corpus, not twelve independent model reviewers or human sessions. Attention budgets constrain what a persona would reasonably inspect; browser execution time is not human reading time. Deep journeys combine actual clicked shared paths with role-specific selection of reached evidence. Direct navigations and connector source reads are marked; their clicks are not falsely counted as a completed human path. Home resets make some routes separate branches rather than one continuous session.

The baseline CLI context had no saved preferences. Dedicated Chrome contexts were ephemeral with no saved storage. Normal motion was used initially; reduced motion was a separate check. No analytics consent was enabled. External endpoints were read with GET only. Initial sandbox HTTP failures and harness selector/menu failures were resolved or retained in ignored attempt logs; they are not public-site outages.

Browser: Chrome 155.0.8059.85 on the local macOS host via existing external Playwright, headless isolated contexts. Viewports: 390×844, 768×1024, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. Normal initial motion; separate reduced-motion run. No new runtime dependencies. The existing public audit uses bounded batches of six; browser pages and external links were inspected sequentially. No load test or vulnerability scan.

Primary corpus: `output/playwright/employer-experience-v1/` (ignored). `browser.json` indexes captured routes, screenshots, actions, console and request evidence. `followup.json` records corrected mobile interactions, five axe checks, résumé rendering and public hashes. `public-source.json` / `public-code.json` preserve sampled public repository material. `initial-home-snapshot.yml`, `predeclared-rubrics.json`, retained attempt logs and `scored-personas.json` make the reasoning traceable. The portable index is `verification/employer-experience-audit-v1.json`; raw evidence is local, not committed.

## Adversarial review and limitations

The skeptical pass challenged the favorable conclusions: 108 HTTP successes do not bind a provider SHA; public source does not demonstrate production operation; no-JS/axe checks do not certify accessibility; saved NAAE outputs do not demonstrate a live generative studio; game campaign size does not imply backend scale or playability; critical-question abstention does not prove useful answer quality. Self-reported résumé and case details are not independent employment verification. Honest caveats deserve trust credit but do not erase missing role evidence. No principal-level rejection is inferred simply from broad technologies or AI-assisted development.

The audit uses one browser engine, one local network/host and a shared model-authored evidence interpretation. Source inspection is sampled, with no fresh application tests or full code/security review. No native-language editorial review or human usability study occurred. Raw evidence remains ignored/local; moving only the Markdown files will lose screenshots unless the approved evidence bundle is retained. Previous 161 tests, translation counts and Lighthouse scores remain REPORTED historical context, never transferred to this deployment.
