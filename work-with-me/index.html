Modernisation & delivery

For teams improving a legacy platform while existing business workflows still need to run.

Useful inputs
The current codebase, business rules, consumer dependencies, test coverage and deployment constraints.
Possible deliverables
An agreed scope may include migration options, a phased implementation plan or hands-on delivery of a bounded change.
Next step
Start with the workflow or integration that makes change hardest.

Possible deliverables
